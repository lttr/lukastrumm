Don't hesitate to build your own tool or even a whole program for a single operation. For a woodworker this is a fairly common situation. When there is no tool at hand that fits the job exactly, they make one. A one-off tool improves the precision and elegance of the solution. With an agent, it takes minutes.

For inspiration, here is the "Playground" skill: https://github.com/anthropics/claude-plugins-official/tree/main/plugins/playground and an elegant demonstration of building a one-off tool (the author is a designer, not a developer): https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lkKGQVHrXzE&t=2172s
